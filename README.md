# the-last-floor

you spawn inside a building and your main objective is to find your friend Alex this building contains 10 floors in total but we have only completed the first 3 floors for you to play right now this is an atmospheric horror game where you travel down in an elevator into pitch black darkness you must rely on your torch to see anything but you have to be extremely careful because terrifying entities will move and teleport the exact moment you look away from them

on the first floor which is also known as the lobby you need to collect the torch and then search for various items including notes a tape recorder a keycard a photo and a specific note left behind by Alex

after that on floor 2 you must locate and fix the breaker box to restore the lights to the entire floor once the power is back on you can enter the maintenance room to find the key that grants you elevator access to the third floor

finally on floor 3 you will step out into a massive hallway where you have to find and enter a very specific door that has a joker painting hanging right outside of it that is the end of the current gameplay but we will try our best to complete the rest of the game by week 2


# Ai usage

we generated a photograph of alex using gemini as well as a voice recording of him using elevenlabs


# controls
W A S D to walk around
mouse to look around the environment
E to press buttons open doors pick up items and play tapes
esc is used to uncapture your mouse cursor

# how to play
download the game zip file in itch.io : https://aahil1.itch.io/the-last-floor
extract the folder onto your computer
double click the game exe file to start playing

you can also download the zip file here in godot and then import this project in godot
